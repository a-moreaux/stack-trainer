# Card Stack Trainer

Learn and drill your memorized deck on your iPhone. Mnemonica, Aronson, Si Stebbins, or your own stack.
Study the order, practise card-to-position and position-to-card, test your neighbours, and earn stars
in 30-second challenges. Free. Works without internet. No account, no App Store, no ads.
In English and French.

---

## Install it on your iPhone (about 1 minute)

You don't need a GitHub account and you don't need the App Store.

1. **On your iPhone, open Safari** (the blue compass app). It has to be Safari.
2. **Open this link:**
   ### 👉 [https://a-moreaux.github.io/stack-trainer/](https://a-moreaux.github.io/stack-trainer/)
   You should see a fan of playing cards and a button that says **Choose a stack**.
3. **Tap the Share button.**
   - On iOS 26: tap **⋯** at the bottom right of the screen, then **Share**.
   - On older iPhones: tap the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen.
4. **Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".**
5. **Leave "Open as Web App" switched on, then tap "Add".**
6. **Find the new red card-back icon on your home screen and open it.**
   Open it once while you're connected to the internet. After that it works anywhere, even in airplane mode.

That's it. Choose your stack (or build your own) and start practising.

### Good to know
- **Always open the app from its home-screen icon**, not from Safari. The icon keeps your progress.
- **Your stacks and progress stay on your phone.** Nobody else can see them.
- **Don't delete the icon**: that can erase your progress. To be safe, now and then go to
  **Settings → Save a backup → Copy** and paste the text into the Notes app.
- **New phone?** Save a backup on the old phone, install the app on the new one,
  then **Settings → Restore from a backup** and paste.
- **Updates install themselves** the next time you open the app with internet.

### Something not working?
- **No "Add to Home Screen"?** Make sure you're in Safari, not Chrome or an app's built-in browser
  (for example, a link opened from Instagram or Messenger). Copy the link into Safari.
- **"404" or "page not found"?** Check the link is typed exactly, then try again in a minute.
- **The icon opens a normal Safari page with an address bar?** Remove it and add it again with
  "Open as Web App" switched on.

## Installer sur ton iPhone (en français)

1. Sur ton iPhone, ouvre **Safari**.
2. Ouvre ce lien : **[https://a-moreaux.github.io/stack-trainer/](https://a-moreaux.github.io/stack-trainer/)**
3. Touche **Partager** (iOS 26 : touche **⋯** en bas à droite, puis **Partager**).
4. Fais défiler et touche **Sur l’écran d’accueil**.
5. Laisse activée l’option pour l’ouvrir comme **app web**, puis touche **Ajouter**.
6. Ouvre la nouvelle icône une première fois avec Internet. Ensuite, elle fonctionne partout.

Dans l’app, touche le drapeau 🇫🇷 en haut à gauche pour passer en français.
Pense à faire une sauvegarde de temps en temps : **Réglages → Faire une sauvegarde**.

## For the owner of this page: publishing and updating

You only do this once. Everyone else just uses the link above.

1. Make a free GitHub account: <https://github.com/signup>
2. Create a repository: <https://github.com/new>
   Name it `stack-trainer`, choose **Public**, then **Create repository**.
3. Click **uploading an existing file** and drag in all the app files
   (`index.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon-180.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `README.md`).
   Click **Commit changes**.
4. Open **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment → Source**, choose **Deploy from a branch**,
   then **main** and **/ (root)**, and **Save**.
5. After a minute or two, your address appears at the top of that page:
   <https://a-moreaux.github.io/stack-trainer/>

**To update:** upload the new files the same way. They replace the old ones. The app updates itself
the next time it's opened with internet. **Settings** shows the version at the bottom.
